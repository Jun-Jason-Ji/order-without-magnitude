  the three-seed record (seeds 43/44 for every trained backbone) with its six ERRATA; aggregate
  labels across seeds and the preregistered title rule.
- All decision rules shipped under protocols/ with their SHA-256; receipts scrubbed of local paths.
- Per-item prediction rows remain withheld (they carry tracking-derived references); the 392-item
  synthetic text-probe rows are included.
