## 1. Mechanism of the one-tile image policy (recorded 2026-10-06, after two failed smoke gates and before any seed-42 training, evaluation or probe output of Llama 3.2 Vision existed)

The preregistration pins "max_image_tiles = 1 (processor default 4), tile 560x560; a 960x540 frame becomes one 560x315 view padded to the tile". Setting the processor's `max_image_tiles` to 1 alone does not run: `MllamaForConditionalGeneration` stores its two precomputed tile-position embeddings for the model's `max_num_tiles = 4` slots and adds all four by broadcasting, so a 1-slot `pixel_values` tensor becomes four and the next reshape fails (`RuntimeError: shape '[4, 1600, 1280]' is invalid for input of size 32768000`; smoke of 2026-10-06T04:03Z, all three steps failed at the first forward).

A first amendment kept the processor at 4 tile slots and shrank every frame to a 560-px longest edge so that one slot was real and three were padding (smoke of 2026-10-06T09:17Z): the base evaluation then ran, but training ran out of memory on the 40 GB card inside the vision encoder, because the padded slots are computed like real ones (16 tiles of 560x560 per four-frame item).

Final mechanism, same intent as preregistered: the processor is pinned to `max_image_tiles = 1` (each frame is one 560x560 tile, exactly as the preregistration describes), and the two tile-embedding modules (`MllamaPrecomputedAspectRatioEmbedding`, `MllamaPrecomputedPositionEmbedding`) are patched at import to slice their stored embeddings to the number of tile slots present. For a 1x1 canvas the real tile occupies slot 0 of the padded layout, so the sliced embeddings are the values the standard run applies to that tile; no padded slot is computed. The patch lives in `a100/family_policy_llama32.py`, is installed in training, evaluation and the probe alike, and the preflight asserts `pixel_values` of shape (1, 4, 1, 3, 560, 560), `aspect_ratio_ids == [1, 1, 1, 1]` and that the patch is present. Checked locally on a small random `MllamaVisionModel` against the padded four-slot run of the same frames: the real-tile features agree (differences at the level of the masked-attention numerics, far below the difference between two frames).

No decision rule changes. The failed smoke directories are discarded; the gate is re-run from scratch.
